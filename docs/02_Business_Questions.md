# HR Analytics Project - Business Questions

## Project Objective

Analyze employee attrition patterns and identify the key factors influencing employee turnover to support HR retention and workforce planning strategies.

---

## Business Problem

Employee attrition can lead to increased recruitment costs, loss of organizational knowledge, reduced productivity, and challenges in workforce planning.

The objective of this analysis is to understand which employee characteristics, workplace factors, and career-related factors contribute most to attrition.

---

## Business Questions

### Attrition Overview

1. What is the overall attrition rate?

### Department Analysis

2. Which departments have the highest attrition?

### Job Role Analysis

3. Which job roles experience the highest attrition?

### Demographic Analysis

4. Which age groups are most likely to leave?
5. Does gender influence attrition?
6. Does marital status influence attrition?

### Compensation Analysis

7. Does salary influence attrition?
8. Does salary hike influence attrition?

### Employee Experience Analysis

9. Does job satisfaction influence attrition?
10. Does work-life balance influence attrition?
11. Does overtime contribute to attrition?

### Career Growth Analysis

12. Does promotion history influence attrition?
13. Does tenure influence attrition?

---

## KPI Framework

### Executive KPIs

* Total Employees
* Attrition Count
* Attrition Rate
* Average Age
* Average Monthly Income
* Average Tenure
