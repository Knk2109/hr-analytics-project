### 2. Overall Attrition Rate

* Overall Attrition Rate is 16%
* The organization's attrition rate is moderate, but specific employee segments show significantly higher risk.

### 3. Top 5 Executive Findings

1. **Overtime Is the Strongest Attrition Driver**
   1. 30% attrition (Overtime)
   2. 10% attrition (No Overtime)
   3. Employees working overtime are 3X more likely to leave the organisation.
2. **New Employees are most vulnerable**
   1. 0-2 years = 29% attrition
   2. 10+ years = 8% attrition
   3. Retention rish is concentrated in the first two years.
3. **Younger Employees Leave More Often**
   1. Under 30 = 27%
   2. 40-49 = 9%
   3. Early career employees are significantly at higher risk
4. **Compensation Matters**
   1. Below 5K = 21%
   2. 15k+ = 3%
   3. Higher paid employees show much stronger retention
5. **Employee Experience Matters**
   1. Low job satisfaction = 22%
   2. Poor work-life balance = 31%
   3. Work environment factors are strongly associated with attrition.

### 4. Supporting Insights

* **Insight 1: Younger Employees Are Leaving More Frequently**

| Age Group | Attrition Rate |
| :-------: | -------------: |
| Under 30 |            27% |
|   30-39   |            14% |
|   40-49   |             9% |
|    50+    |            13% |

    **Business Story**

> Employees under 30 exhibit the highest attrition rate at 27%, nearly three times the attrition rate of employees aged 40-49. This suggests that younger employees may be seeking faster career growth, better compensation, or external opportunities.

* **Insight 2: Low Job Satisfaction Drives Attrition**

| Job Satisfaction | Attrition Rate |
| ---------------- | -------------: |
| 1 (Lowest)       |            22% |
| 2                |            16% |
| 3                |            16% |
| 4 (Highest)      |            11% |

    **Business Story**

> Employees reporting the lowest job satisfaction experience attrition rates twice as high as employees with the highest job satisfaction.

* **Insight 3: Poor Work-Life Balance Is a Risk Factor**

| Work-Life Balance | Attrition Rate |
| ----------------- | -------------: |
| 1                 |            31% |
| 2                 |            16% |
| 3                 |            14% |
| 4                 |            17% |

**Business Story**

> Employees reporting the poorest work-life balance show a 31% attrition rate, nearly double the company average.

* I**nsight 4: Compensation Has a Strong Impact**

| Income Band | Attrition Rate |
| ----------- | -------------: |
| Below 5K    |            21% |
| 5K-10K      |            11% |
| 10K-15K     |            13% |
| 15K+        |             3% |

**Business Story**

> Employees earning below 5K experience the highest attrition, while employees earning above 15K show only 3% attrition.

* **Insight 5: Attrition Happens Early**

| Tenure     | Attrition Rate |
| ---------- | -------------: |
| 0-2 Years  |            29% |
| 3-5 Years  |            13% |
| 6-10 Years |            12% |
| 10+ Years  |             8% |

**Business Story**

> Employees within their first two years are at the highest risk of leaving, with a 29% attrition rate. Attrition steadily declines as tenure increases

* **Insight 6: Education Field and Attrition**

| Education Field  | Attrition Rate |
| ---------------- | -------------: |
| Human Resources  |            25% |
| Technical Degree |            24% |
| Marketing        |            22% |
| Life Sciences    |            14% |
| Other            |            13% |
| Medical          |            13% |

**Business Story**

> Employees from Human Resources, Technical Degree, and Marketing educational backgrounds show the highest attrition rates, ranging from 22% to 25%, compared to 13-14% for Medical and Life Sciences employees.HR and Technical Degree backgrounds show elevated attrition rates, although some categories have relatively small employee populations and should be interpreted cautiously.

* **Insight 7: Marital Status and Attrition**

| Marital Status | Attrition Rate |
| -------------- | -------------: |
| Single         |            25% |
| Married        |            12% |
| Divorced       |            10% |

**Business Story**

> Single employees experience attrition rates more than twice those of married employees and 2.5 times those of divorced employees.
