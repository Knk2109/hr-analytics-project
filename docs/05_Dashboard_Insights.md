# HR Analytics Dashboard Insights

## Dashboard Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard was developed using PostgreSQL and Power BI to analyze employee attrition patterns and identify the key drivers influencing employee turnover.

The project follows a complete analytics lifecycle:

* Data Audit and Quality Assessment
* Data Cleaning and Preparation
* Star Schema Data Modeling
* Fact and Dimension Table Design
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* KPI Development
* Power BI Dashboard Creation
* Executive Insights and Recommendations

The dashboard enables HR leaders and business stakeholders to monitor employee attrition trends and make data-driven workforce decisions.

# Executive Findings

## 1. Overtime is the Strongest Attrition Driver

Employees working overtime exhibit an attrition rate of 30.53%, compared to only 10.44% for employees not working overtime.

### Business Impact

Excessive workload and employee burnout may significantly increase employee turnover risk.

---

## 2. Younger Employees Have Higher Attrition

Employees under 30 years of age experience an attrition rate of 27.91%, which is substantially higher than other age groups.

### Business Impact

Early-career employees may be seeking faster career growth, better compensation, or external opportunities.

---

## 3. Attrition is Concentrated in the First Two Years

Employees with 0-2 years of tenure show an attrition rate of 29.82%.

Attrition steadily declines as employee tenure increases.

### Business Impact

The organization is losing employees during the early stages of their employment lifecycle.

---

## 4. Compensation Influences Retention

Employees earning below 5K per month exhibit a 21.76% attrition rate, while employees earning above 15K show only 3.76% attrition.

### Business Impact

Compensation appears to be a significant factor affecting employee retention.

---

## 5. Employee Experience Impacts Attrition

Employees with:

* Low Job Satisfaction (22.84%)
* Poor Work-Life Balance (31.25%)

demonstrate significantly higher attrition rates.

### Business Impact

Employee engagement and workplace experience directly influence employee retention outcomes.

---

## 6. Entry-Level Employees Face Higher Attrition Risk

Job Level 1 employees show an attrition rate of 26.34%, substantially higher than employees in senior job levels.

### Business Impact

Retention challenges are concentrated among entry-level employees.

---

# High-Risk Employee Profile

Based on the analysis, employees with the highest probability of attrition typically share the following characteristics:

* Under 30 Years Old
* Single
* Job Level 1
* Monthly Income Below 5K
* Less Than 2 Years of Tenure
* Frequently Working Overtime
* Low Job Satisfaction
* Poor Work-Life Balance

---

# Recommendations

## Recommendation 1

Strengthen onboarding and first-year employee retention programs.

## Recommendation 2

Reduce excessive overtime and monitor workload distribution.

## Recommendation 3

Review compensation strategies for entry-level employees.

## Recommendation 4

Improve employee engagement and job satisfaction initiatives.

## Recommendation 5

Develop targeted retention programs for younger employees and high-risk workforce segments.
